Quick Start Handbook
1. Planning a Journey: Enter origin and destination stations into the Journey Planner. The algorithm computes 3 prioritized alternatives factoring in walking distance, transfer wait times and current corridor congestion.
2. Live Vehicle Tracking: The Live Map visualizes simulated real-time positions for all buses in service. Click any bus icon to open the telemetry inspector showing speed, next stop and passenger occupancy.
3. Understanding Alerts: Incidents are categorized as Severe (service disruption), Moderate (corridor detours), or Advisory (minor platform adjustments).

Frequently Asked Questions
Vehicle telemetry in this demonstration environment is refreshed every 4 seconds via simulated GPS coordinate updates. In production deployments, data connects to municipal automated vehicle location (AVL) GTFS Realtime feeds.
The delay indicator compares the estimated arrival time with the scheduled timetable published in the general transit feed. Positive values indicate delay due to congestion or detours.
Walking links use pedestrian street networks calibrated to a standard 4.5 km/h walking speed, incorporating pedestrian signals and step-free accessibility preferences where configured.
